01 · The Lore

The Lore

Every meme coin needs a story. Mine is a black cat who refuses to care.

  1. 01

    The Market Panics

    Charts pump, charts dump. Everyone screams in the group chat.

  2. 02

    Hex Just Sits There

    Paw on a coin, hat on, zero reaction. She has seen this before.

  3. 03

    One Slow Blink

    Not impressed. Not bothered. The blink is the whole reply.

  4. 04

    Still Holding

    Hex never sells. That one line became the tagline and the brand.
