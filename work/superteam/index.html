Faq

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Superteam Ukraine

What is Superteam Ukraine?

A Solana community where Web3 startups are launched, grants and investments are secured, people learn, and earn in the crypto economy.

What happened to Kumeka Team?

Solana in Ukraine started with the Kumeka Team. That same community now grows as Superteam Ukraine.

Why should I join Superteam Ukraine?

You connect with founders, developers, designers, and marketers building in the Solana ecosystem, and you can earn $100-$2K+ per task through Superteam Earn bounties.

What kind of support can I get for my projects?

Instagrants of $1k-$10k, Ukrainian track prizes up to $10K, seed investment from Colosseum, and a free startup course through Solana Startup Terminal.

What else can I get in Superteam Ukraine?

Regular meetups, workshops and community events, university programs, and internship support across Ukraine.
